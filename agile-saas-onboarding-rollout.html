Agile SaaS Onboarding Rollout

A project by Vimlesh Sonawane, Project Manager. Simulated scenario, not an employer project.

What I created

I created an end-to-end onboarding rollout plan for a B2B partner/client program, plus a working demo: a charter, backlog, sprint plans, RAID log, retrospective, and resume bullets.

Live demo

Try the interactive Agile board: product backlog with user stories, points and acceptance criteria, Sprint 1–3 boards in To Do / In Progress / In Review / Done, remaining-work tracking, RAID risks, and roadmap. You can also add your own story to the backlog in your browser session.

Open the live Agile board demo

What I analysed

I analysed a manual onboarding process that, in the simulated scenario, took 21 days and depended on scattered checklists and email follow-ups. The bottlenecks were unclear ownership, late security review, no single progress view, and no training handoff criteria.

Significance

This project shows scope, schedule, risk, stakeholder, and data-informed decision-making in a form a hiring manager can open and discuss, with a 10-day pilot target for the simulated flow.

Tools and methodologies used

Agile and Scrum across 3 two-week sprints; Jira-style backlog and sprint tracking; Confluence-style documentation; RAID risk management; stakeholder analysis; retrospectives.
