# Research & Coursework

Real R scripts and reports from Northeastern coursework, referenced directly
from the portfolio site's Projects section.

- **aly6000/** — ALY 6000, Introduction to Analytics (6 projects: R fundamentals,
  World Happiness EDA, book publishing trends, diabetes risk factors, probability
  fundamentals, and probability distributions)
- **aly6010/** — ALY 6010, Probability Theory and Introductory Statistics
  (t-tests, airline delay EDA, DC crime analysis, Maryland labor data,
  NYC crash regression, NYC restaurant inspection regression, and the final
  project on social media addiction)

Each script (`.R`) has a matching report (`.pdf` or `.docx`) with the write-up,
methodology, and findings.
