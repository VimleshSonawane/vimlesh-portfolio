Cutting staff repayment time from 20 days to 9

Staff pay for work trips themselves, then claim the money back. In a real university’s approval process, the slowest 1 in 10 claims took over 20 days to be repaid. As project manager, I ran a 23-day improvement project: mapped the approval steps, found three bottlenecks, and designed fixes that bring that wait down to 9 days in a what-if model.

01

The problem: staff were out of pocket for weeks

In 2018, the university’s domestic travel-expense approval process took a median of 8.05 days from submission to payment, but 1 in 10 paid claims took more than 20.25 days. 13.14% of submitted claims were rejected at least once, and rejected claims took 1.8× as long to pay. Payments were released only on Mondays and Thursdays, so the wait before payment was the longest step in the process.
What

Slow, unpredictable repayment of staff travel expenses

Roll out in two waves, then keep watching the numbers

Proposed roadmap and control plan (D7), built in Smartsheet.

Wave 1 · Jan 2027 · quick wins

Simple changes first

  • Claim checklist with an amount > 0 rule (F2)
  • Approval reminders after 3 days (F3)
  • Backup approver, reason codes, logging admin time
Wave 2 · Jan–Mar 2027 · major projects

Bigger changes after a review

  • Daily payment runs (F1)
  • Block payment without final approval (F8)
  • Full review against the targets
